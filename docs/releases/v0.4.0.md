# ClipCraft 0.4.0

A calmer, redesigned window, a much safer library, and a real editor: choose many clips at once,
trim and caption with proper controls, export in any picture shape, and see the same kind of message
everywhere.

## What's new

- **Choose many clips at once.** **Select** on the Clips page: click, Shift-click for a range, drag a
  box over empty space, Ctrl or ⌘ and A for all. Then favorite, put in a collection or delete.
  Delete names the clips, leaves favorites out unless you say otherwise, and sends them to the Recycle
  Bin when you close the message that offers **Undo** (or **Delete now**). **Esc** clears your choice
  first and leaves choosing mode the second time.
- **Safer by default.** The storage limit never touches favorites, clips in a collection or clips in
  an open edit, and moves old clips to the Recycle Bin. Every saved clip is reopened and checked, and
  you are warned when the disk is getting full. Settings and the library keep rolling backups and
  recover from a damaged file. After repeated failed starts ClipCraft opens with default settings and
  asks before it changes anything of yours. ClipCraft asks before quitting while the replay holds
  footage. Discarding the editor draft asks first.
- **A redesigned window.** A calmer capture bar, picture cards in three sizes or a list, clips grouped
  by day, your own order by dragging cards, a coloured mark for each game, and short **?** buttons
  instead of paragraphs of hints. The library's **Newest first** menu and the clip's folder button
  were redone, and the folder button opens the folder with the clip selected.
- **One look for every message.** Saved, failed, warning and information messages now follow one
  design guide (`docs/notifications.md`): a coloured badge, the same corners, the same slide in and
  out, and a thin line that shows how long a message stays (8 seconds for good news, 16 for warnings,
  30 for errors; a message with Undo stays until you close it). The "clip saved" pop-up in the corner
  of the screen is larger, has rounded corners on Windows 11, and slides in smoothly even while
  ClipCraft is minimized.
- **Editor controls.** Loop, previous and next cut, frame step (by the clip's own frame rate), time to
  the hundredth, Home and End, timeline zoom (+, −, 0, Ctrl and the mouse wheel), and a segment panel
  with In, Out, volume and mute.
- **Any picture shape.** Preview and export in 16:9, 21:9, 4:3, 3:2, 5:4, 1:1, 4:5, 2:3, 3:4, 9:16,
  9:21 or any shape from 1:4 to 4:1, fitted, blurred, filled or stretched. The viewer now shows the
  shape you are working in, and the export window says where the file will go and lets you pick
  another folder.
- **Text on clips.** Sans, Serif or Mono in four weights, italic, underlined, capitals, spacing,
  shadow, outline, a dark box and see-through text, in any colour, with six ready-made styles.
- **Experimental (Settings → Experimental, off by default).** Pause while a password manager, a
  program you name, or the lock screen is up; keep the replay while paused.
- **Sturdier recording.** Stopping never hangs for more than five seconds; a slow sound device no
  longer stalls the others; a crash inside a recording is reported at once; only one ClipCraft runs at
  a time; and a window that cannot open now says why instead of vanishing.

## Downloads

- **Windows:** `ClipCraft-Setup-0.4.0.exe` (installs for your user, Start menu links, uninstaller)
  or `clipcraft-0.4.0-windows-x86_64-portable.zip` (unzip anywhere and run `bin\clipcraft.exe`).
- **Linux:** `clipcraft-0.4.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`.
- **Command line tool:** its own release, `cli-v0.4.0`.

## Important limits

- The new windows and messages were checked in the running Windows app, not yet on a second
  machine, on Windows 10 or on Linux/Wayland. The rounded pop-up corners need Windows 11; elsewhere
  the pop-up has a plain outline.
- Downloads are unsigned. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning. Check downloads against each
  release's SHA256SUMS.txt.
- Real games, other graphics cards and macOS are not fully tested; `docs/gaps.md` lists what is
  verified and what is not.

No media, logs, screenshots, or bug reports are uploaded automatically.
